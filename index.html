Questions

Does my child need a phone?

+

No. Mimo works without your child carrying a phone or using a screen.

Who can connect with my child?

+

Only people with a Connection Charm who have been approved by the parent can become part of your child's Mimo circle.

What is a Connection Charm and how do Charms work?

+

Each charm represents someone important. Press their charm to send a small signal — a simple way to say, “I'm thinking about you.”

Can we add more people later?

+

Yes. Add Connection Charms as your child's circle grows. The bracelet can hold up to five interchangeable charms, so their connections can evolve over time.

Does Mimo have location awareness?

+

Yes. Mimo is being designed with location awareness to give parents additional peace of mind while their child is away.

Does Mimo require a subscription?

+

Yes. Mimo is being designed with its own cellular connection so it can work away from home without your child needing a phone. Cellular connectivity will require a subscription.

Is Mimo available now?

+

Not yet. We're currently building Mimo and preparing for family testing. Join the waitlist to follow our progress and be among the first to know when it's available.
